Wealth Calculator

Track your past deposits, plan future withdrawals, and calculate your exact portfolio value at any specific date using daily compounding.

How to Use This Calculator?

  • Set Your Target Date: Select the date (e.g., Retirement age) when you want to check your total wealth.
  • Add Deposits (+): Enter the date and amount of money you have invested in the past or plan to invest in the future.
  • Add Withdrawals (-): Need cash for emergencies or goals? Add a withdrawal entry. The calculator will automatically adjust the loss of future compounding.
  • Calculate: Hit the button to see the final value of your portfolio on your target date based on your expected return.
