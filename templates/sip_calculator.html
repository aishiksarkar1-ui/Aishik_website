The estimated annual return rates, projected amounts and scenario probabilities shown here are assumptions informed by past performance and are provided only to illustrate possible outcomes. Actual results may differ materially. The Bull, Normal and Bear scenarios do not cover every possible outcome; the Bear scenario is not a worst-case limit, and losses may be greater.
