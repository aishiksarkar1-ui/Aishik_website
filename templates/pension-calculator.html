YOUR LIFE CHANGES. YOUR PLAN CAN TOO.

Build Your Plan Around Your Life

You do not need to invest the same amount throughout your working life. Start with what you can afford today, add more when a loan ends, or invest a future maturity amount. Use the controls below to build that timeline.

For example

Invest ₹5,000 per month now. When a loan ends after 5 years, add another ₹10,000 per month. You can also invest a one-time amount after 7 years. To pause a SIP, end one phase and start another later.

Start and end years are measured from today. SIP phases add to any other SIP running at the same time. Payments start at the beginning of the chosen start year and stop before the end year. Money already invested keeps growing after a SIP stops. The linked planner above adjusts today’s SIP/lumpsum to include these future contributions.

You can also set an amount to leave for your family at your pension-end age. The planner will then aim to fund both your pension and that legacy, instead of targeting a zero closing balance.*
